What Actually Affects Your Completion Rate

Completion rate is the metric most Pros feel is unfair — and that feeling usually comes from a misunderstanding of what it actually measures. This post lays it out plainly.

---

The formula

Jobs completed ÷ jobs scheduled.

That's it. If you were scheduled for 10 jobs this quarter and completed 8, your completion rate is 80%.

---

What counts against it — and this is the part that trips people up

Every unfinished job counts. That includes:

- Jobs you cancelled
- Jobs a customer skipped
- Jobs that got rescheduled and didn't complete
- Jobs you couldn't access because the gate was locked
- Jobs the customer paused or cancelled

It doesn't matter why the job didn't finish. The system doesn't separate "Pro's fault" from "customer's fault" for completion rate purposes. All of it counts.

This is the one that makes Pros feel it's broken:

"I have never had an incomplete job... yet because of customers skipping service at their discretion, my completion rate tanks."

That Pro isn't wrong about what's happening. They're just running into how completion rate is designed. The thresholds account for it — keep reading.

---

What does NOT count against completion rate

Customer-initiated cancellations do NOT affect your cancellation rate. That's a different metric. Cancellation rate only counts cancellations you caused — property drops you initiated, Pro-cancelled jobs.

So: a customer cancels → your completion rate takes a small hit. Your cancellation rate does not.

---

Why the thresholds are set where they are

Gold requires 55% completion. Platinum requires 70%.

Those numbers look low because they're built to absorb the reality of the job. Most Pros skip 5–10% of scheduled mows for reasons outside their control — weather, customer skips, access problems. The buffer is intentional.

A Pro doing everything right can still miss a job here and there. The thresholds reflect that.

---

Your tier is set by your lowest metric

This is the part people miss. Completion rate doesn't exist in a vacuum. Your tier is determined by whichever of your three metrics — rating, completion rate, cancellation rate — is the weakest.

A Pro with a 4.9 rating and a 2% cancellation rate can still be capped at Silver if their completion rate is 42%. One weak number is the ceiling, not the floor.

---

What actually helps your completion rate

- Complete jobs on the day they're scheduled. Rescheduled jobs that don't complete still count.
- Don't let skips pile up. One or two uncontrollable skips is normal. A pattern of skipped jobs in a short window will show up.
- Skip jobs correctly through the app — don't just not show up. A properly reported skip documents the reason and gives you the best path to a trip fee where you're eligible.
- Review your messages before driving out. If a customer told you to skip in advance, that's already a miss on your completion rate before you've left the house. Seeing it early lets you plan around it.
- Don't drop properties impulsively. Dropping a customer counts toward completion rate (and cancellation rate). If a property is genuinely a problem, go through the reporting flow rather than dropping it outright.

---

How to check your current rate

App → Settings → Performance. Metrics refresh nightly. Your tier updates weekly (new scale) or quarterly (engaged scale), so your live completion rate may look different from the number that set your current tier.

---

Quick reference

| Situation | Affects completion rate? | Affects cancellation rate? |
|---|---|---|
| You cancelled the job | Yes | Yes |
| Customer cancelled | Yes | No |
| Customer skipped | Yes | No |
| Job rescheduled (not completed) | Yes | No |
| Locked gate / couldn't access | Yes | No |
| Job completed on time | Positive | Positive |
