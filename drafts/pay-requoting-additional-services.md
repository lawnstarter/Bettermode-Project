# Adding Services on the Spot — How to Re-Quote Through the App

You show up to mow, and the customer asks if you can also trim the hedges or clean up some debris while you're there. Or you look at the property and realize there's additional work that would make the job complete.

That extra work has value — and there's a way to capture it through the platform instead of working it out off-app.

---

## Why this matters

Doing extra work on a job that wasn't in the listing — without going through the app — creates a few problems:

- You're performing uncompensated labor if the customer doesn't pay you directly
- It sets a precedent that the customer may expect again next time
- It moves the transaction off-platform in a way that can create disputes with no documentation

The in-app re-quote flow is how you add services to a job and get compensated correctly.

---

## How to re-quote for additional services

When you're on-site and a customer requests (or you identify) additional work:

1. **Don't start the extra work yet.** Get the scope clear first.
2. **Use the app's re-quote or additional services flow** to add the service and updated price.
3. **The customer confirms the updated quote** before you proceed.
4. **Then complete the full job** — original service plus the addition.

This keeps everything in the platform, creates a record, and ensures you're paid for what you actually did.

---

## Common additional services worth quoting

- Debris cleanup beyond standard
- Edging or trimming that wasn't in the original service
- Leaf blowing or haul-away
- Extra passes for overgrown areas not captured in the listing

If you're doing it, it should be in the job. If it's in the job, it should be priced.

---

## What if a customer doesn't want to go through the app?

Some customers will offer to pay you directly for the extra work. This is understandable, but it comes with risk on your end: no platform record, no dispute protection, and potential issues if the customer later denies agreeing to the extra work.

The app flow exists to protect you as much as it protects the platform. Using it is the safer path.

---

## If the re-quote flow isn't working

If you're having trouble accessing the re-quote feature on a specific job, contact support before leaving the property. Don't absorb the extra work for free or handle it off-platform out of frustration with the flow.

Report the issue so it can be fixed — and so you're not left eating the cost.
