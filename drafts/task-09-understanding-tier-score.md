Understanding Your Tier Score — What Moves It, What Doesn't

First 60 Days | Post 3 of 4

---

New Pros aren't measured the same way as experienced Pros. Here's what's actually being tracked when you're starting out, and what to focus on.

---

You're on a different scale

If you've been on the platform less than 3 months or have fewer than 20 mowing jobs, you're on the new Pro scale: Blue, Intro, or Red.

Experienced Pros are on a different scale (Platinum/Gold/Silver/Bronze). Their metrics work differently. Don't compare your numbers to what you see from long-term Pros.

Your tier updates every week while you're new. That's faster than the quarterly updates engaged Pros get — good weeks show up quickly.

---

What's being measured

Three things, all specific to the new scale:

1. Review Surplus — not your average rating

The formula: add up all your ratings, then subtract (4 × number of ratings).

- 5-star rating: pushes your surplus up by +1
- 4-star rating: keeps it exactly flat
- 3-star or below: pulls your surplus down

You need a surplus of +3 or higher to reach Blue.

Example: you've received three 5-star ratings. That's 15 total, minus (4 × 3 = 12) = surplus of +3. You're at the Blue threshold.

2. Completion rate — jobs completed ÷ jobs scheduled

All unfinished jobs count, regardless of reason. Customer skips, locked gates, weather — everything counts. Blue requires 55% or higher.

3. Cancellation rate — Pro-initiated cancellations only

Customer cancellations don't count here. Only cancellations you caused. Blue requires 8% or lower.

Your tier is set by your lowest metric. One weak number is what holds you back.

---

What the data shows about your first 10 jobs

The signal in your ratings shows up early. By the time you have 3 ratings — usually around your 7th job — your trajectory is already visible:

- Averaging 4.8 or higher at 3 ratings: 65% chance of reaching Blue
- Averaging 4.0–4.5: 35% chance
- Averaging below 3.5: 8.5% chance

You don't need a perfect record. You need consistent, solid work at the start. The early jobs set the pattern.

---

What moves it in the right direction

- Complete jobs on the day they're scheduled. Rescheduling or skipping hurts completion rate.
- Respond to customers through the app. Responsiveness affects how customers rate you.
- Take before and after photos on every job. Customers who see photo documentation are less likely to dispute — disputed jobs create problems that can cascade into lower ratings.
- Show up. The single biggest predictor of Blue attainment is whether a Pro keeps completing jobs in their first two weeks.

---

What doesn't move it the way people expect

- Customers skipping a service does NOT affect your cancellation rate — but it does lower your completion rate slightly
- Working hard doesn't guarantee 5-star ratings — customer communication and consistency matter as much as quality of the mow
- Checking your metrics every day won't change them. They update nightly; tier updates weekly.

---

Your one action

Check your Performance screen now: App → Settings → Performance. See your current surplus, completion rate, and cancellation rate. Know where you are.
