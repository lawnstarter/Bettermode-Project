## HOW THIS WORKS

1. Post the thread intro below in Ask the Community, tagged to (Pro)
2. (Pro) answers replies as they come in — no schedule required
3. After 7–10 days (or when activity slows), compile the best Q&As into a "You asked, we answered" Pro Academy post
4. Archive the thread — it becomes a searchable peer resource over time

---

## WHY THIS FORMAT WORKS

New Pros trust other Pros more than they trust HQ. A Gold or Platinum Pro answering "how do I move up?" or "what do you do when a customer cancels last minute?" carries more weight than any official post. This thread gives that credibility a permanent home.

Ideal participant: someone who has been on the platform long enough to have real experience across the ups and downs — tier movement, difficult customers, slow seasons, building their own route. Gold or Platinum. Willing to be honest, not just positive.
