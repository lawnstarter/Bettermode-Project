**How the Tier System Actually Works**

This comes up constantly, so let's just lay it out plainly. No jargon, no runaround.

---

**There are two separate tier worlds**

If you're new (less than 3 months in, or fewer than 20 mowing jobs), you're on the **New tier scale**: Blue, Intro, or Red.

Once you've been here 3+ months AND have 20+ mowing jobs, you move to the **Engaged tier scale**: Platinum, Gold, Silver, or Bronze.

They're measured differently. If you're new and confused why your "rating" isn't what you expected — you're not being measured on average rating yet. Keep reading.

---

**What actually gets measured**

*If you're on the New scale (Blue/Intro/Red):*

Three things:
1. **Review Surplus** — not your average rating. The formula is: add up all your ratings, subtract (4 × number of ratings). Every 5-star pushes this number up. Every 4-star keeps it flat. Anything below 4 pulls it down. You need a surplus of +3 or higher to reach Blue.
2. **Completion rate** — jobs completed ÷ jobs scheduled. Blue requires 55%+.
3. **Cancellation rate** — Pro-initiated cancellations only. Blue requires 8% or less.

Your tier updates **every week** when you're new — so good weeks show up fast.

*If you're on the Engaged scale (Platinum/Gold/Silver/Bronze):*

Same three metrics, but now it's **average rating** instead of surplus. And your tier updates **quarterly** — so it moves slower, which actually protects you.

| Tier | Rating | Completion | Cancellation |
|------|--------|------------|--------------|
| Platinum | 4.8+ | 70%+ | 5% or less |
| Gold | 4.5+ | 55%+ | 8% or less |
| Silver | 4.0+ | 40%+ | 12% or less |
| Bronze | Below Silver | Below Silver | Below Silver |

Your tier is set by your **lowest** metric. One weak number is what holds you back, not the average of all three.

---

**The thing most people get wrong**

A lot of Pros assume customer skips and customer cancellations hit their score the same way. They don't.

- **Completion rate** — YES, customer skips count here. If a customer skips service and the job doesn't get completed, your completion rate takes a small hit. This is the frustrating one, and it's real.
- **Cancellation rate** — Customer-initiated cancellations do NOT count here. Only cancellations you caused count toward this metric.

The reason completion rate has a buffer built in: most Pros skip 5–10% of jobs naturally. The thresholds account for that. Gold only requires 55% completion — not 100%, not 90%, 55%. That's meaningful room.

---

**What to focus on if you're new**

Your first 10 jobs are the window that matters most. Here's what the data shows:

- At 3 ratings (usually around your 7th job), your trajectory is already visible
- Pros averaging 4.5+ at that point have a 53–66% chance of reaching Blue
- Pros averaging 4.8+ have a 65% chance

You don't need a perfect record. You need consistent, solid work early.

The single most important action: **take photos on every job, complete jobs on the day they're scheduled, and respond to customers through the app.** That covers all three metrics.

---

**Why your tier matters beyond the label**

Higher tier = you see available jobs first. Blue new Pros get job notifications before Silver engaged Pros. Red Pros don't receive text notifications at all — just app and email.

This is why tier recovery is hard once you slip. Fewer jobs → fewer chances to improve metrics → tier stays low.

---

**Where to check your score**

App → Settings → Performance. Your metrics update nightly, even if your tier only updates weekly or quarterly.
