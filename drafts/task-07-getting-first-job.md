Getting Your First Job — What to Expect

First 60 Days | Post 1 of 4

---

If you're wondering why you don't have a job yet: this is normal. Here's how the system works and what you can do to get moving faster.

---

How jobs get distributed

Jobs aren't assigned to you randomly. When a job opens up, the system notifies Pros in a specific order based on tier and proximity. As a new Pro, you're on the Intro tier by default when you start.

The queue works like this:
1. Platinum Pros see jobs first (nearby, then farther out)
2. Gold Pros next
3. Blue new Pros — you get ahead of Silver engaged Pros here
4. Silver Pros
5. Intro Pros
6. Bronze Pros

New Pros on the Intro tier are toward the end of the line — which means in active markets, jobs get claimed before the notification reaches you. This is why the first job can feel slow to arrive.

The fastest path to seeing more jobs sooner: get to Blue.

---

What Blue means for new Pros

Blue is the top tier on the new Pro scale. It jumps ahead of Silver in the job queue — meaning you see jobs before established engaged Pros do. It's also achievable faster than most Pros expect.

Blue requirements (for new Pros):
- Review surplus of +3 or higher (more on this in Post 3)
- 55% or higher completion rate
- 8% or lower cancellation rate

Tier updates every week when you're new. Good work shows up fast.

---

What to do right now

Accept the first job that comes in. First-job completion is one of the most important moments in your first 60 days — not because of a single metric, but because it starts your track record. Pros who complete their first job on the day it's scheduled are significantly more likely to still be active by their 10th job.

Check that your settings are correct:
- Location services on, set to "always" for the LawnStarter app
- Text notifications enabled (Intro Pros get text alerts)
- Service area and availability set accurately in the app

If you've been waiting more than a few days with no offers: check your service area. If it's too narrow or you're in a lower-volume market, that's the most likely cause. Contact support if something looks off.

---

The first few jobs are the ones that matter most

Of every 100 new Pros who start, about 56 are still completing jobs by their 7th. The ones who make it are usually doing one thing well: showing up and completing what's scheduled.

You don't need to be perfect. You need to be consistent.
