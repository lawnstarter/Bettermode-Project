## HOW THIS WORKS

1. Post the thread intro below in Ask the Community, tagged to the designated person
2. The person answers replies as they come in — no schedule required, just check it when they have a moment
3. After 7–10 days (or when reply activity slows), compile the best Q&As into a "You asked, we answered" Pro Academy post
4. Archive the thread (don't delete) — it becomes searchable over time
5. Rotate to the next department the following month
