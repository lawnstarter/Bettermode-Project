## TOPIC BRIEF

Article: How to Set Up (and Actually Use) Your Google Business Profile as a Lawn Care Pro

---

Target audience

Lawn care professionals — both new to the business and established operators who haven't claimed their GBP yet. Applies to LawnStarter Pros and independent operators.

---

Why this topic, why now

Two separate Pros posted questions in our community this month asking about Google reviews — one trying to rank locally, one wanting to share their rating with prospective customers. Neither got a good answer. That question will keep coming up.

Beyond community demand: "Google Business Profile lawn care" and related searches ("how to get Google reviews lawn care," "lawn care local SEO") are genuine Pro-intent queries with low competition in the lawn care content vertical. We don't have anything ranking for them.

---

Primary keyword targets (suggested — Matei's team should validate)

- how to set up google business profile lawn care
- google business profile lawn care pro
- how to get google reviews lawn care
- lawn care local seo

---

What the article should cover

1. Why GBP matters for a lawn care business — local pack ranking, what customers see when they search your name, social proof for new customers
2. Step-by-step setup — claiming the profile, choosing the right service category, adding service areas (not a storefront address), adding photos
3. Getting reviews without violating Google policy — how to ask correctly, timing the ask (right after a good job), what not to say
4. Using your profile actively — responding to reviews, posting seasonal updates, adding services
5. One practical tip specific to LawnStarter Pros: your platform rating is a starting point, but it lives behind a login. A GBP review is public and searchable. Both matter.

---

Suggested angle / headline options

- How to Set Up Your Google Business Profile as a Lawn Care Pro (and Start Showing Up Locally)
- The Lawn Care Pro's Guide to Google Reviews (and Why They're Worth More Than You Think)
- Get Found Locally: Google Business Profile Setup for Lawn Care Professionals

---

Internal data we can offer (optional, if useful for the article)

- Community signal: Pros in our Bettermode community are actively asking about Google reviews — shows real practitioner demand, not just SEO demand
- If the research team wants a study hook: we could look at correlation between GBP presence and LawnStarter Pro tenure / Blue attainment — but not required for the article to work

---

Suggested length and format

1,200–1,800 words. Step-by-step how-to with screenshots or illustrations if possible. One clear CTA at the end (claim your GBP if you haven't, or audit your existing one).

Not a study story — straightforward practical guide format.
