Getting to Blue — What It Means and How to Get There

First 60 Days | Post 4 of 4

---

Blue is the goal for new Pros. Here's what it unlocks, what it requires, and how to get there.

---

What Blue actually gives you

Blue is the highest tier on the new Pro scale. The most important thing it unlocks: you see available jobs before Silver engaged Pros do.

That's not a small thing. The full queue order puts Blue new Pros ahead of Silver Pros who've been on the platform for years. More jobs seen = more chances to build your schedule.

You also get:
- Text notifications for available jobs (same as Gold and Platinum)
- 1 trip fee per property per quarter
- 10 jobs per day capacity

Intro Pros get text notifications too, but the job queue position is where Blue makes the real difference.

---

The three thresholds

All three must be met. Your tier is capped at the weakest one.

| Metric | Blue requires |
|---|---|
| Review surplus | +3 or higher |
| Completion rate | 55% or higher |
| Cancellation rate | 8% or lower |

Review surplus is the one most new Pros focus on — and it's where the biggest gains happen early. See Post 3 for how the surplus formula works.

---

How fast can you get there?

Faster than most Pros expect.

Your tier updates every week when you're new. If you hit all three thresholds in a given week, you'll be in Blue the following week.

The data on what predicts Blue attainment:
- Averaging 4.8+ on your first 3 ratings: 65% of Pros in that range reach Blue
- Averaging 4.5+: 53–66% reach Blue
- Averaging 4.0–4.4: 35% reach Blue — but 63% get there eventually

The first 7 jobs are when most Pros accumulate their first 3 ratings. That's the window. What you do in those jobs sets your trajectory.

---

The practical checklist

These are the things that consistently separate Pros who reach Blue from those who don't:

✓ Show up on the day the job is scheduled
✓ Take before and after photos on every job
✓ Complete the full scope — don't leave sections undone
✓ Respond to customer messages through the app
✓ Report issues (long grass, locked gate, anything unexpected) before marking complete
✓ Don't cancel jobs unless you absolutely have to

That's it. No tricks, no shortcuts. Blue is a consistency score, not a perfection score.

---

What happens after Blue?

Once you've been on the platform 3 months AND have 20+ mowing jobs, you transition to the engaged Pro scale: Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze. Average rating replaces review surplus, and tier updates quarterly instead of weekly.

Blue puts you in a strong position for that transition. Pros who reach Blue are significantly more likely to still be active at job 10 than those who don't.

---

Your one action

Look at your service area in the app. Is it set wide enough to give you access to available jobs? New Pros sometimes set their area too narrow and then wonder why jobs aren't coming in. A 15–20 mile radius from where you're based is a reasonable starting point for most markets.
