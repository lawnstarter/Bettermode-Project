# Pro Discovery — Opportunity Solution Tree
**Source:** Figma board — Pro Discovery, node "Opportunity Mapping" (203-3418)
**Framework:** Teresa Torres Opportunity Solution Tree
**Scope:** First 30 days Pro experience
**Extracted:** May 1, 2026
**Purpose:** Identify unmet Pro needs to inform Bettermode community content and product roadmap

---

## Desired Outcome

Help new Pros succeed on the LawnStarter platform — completing their first job, understanding how the system works, and building toward Blue tier — within their first 30 days.

---

## What's Already in the Pipeline

Items currently in flight, planned, or on someone's roadmap that touch this opportunity area:

- **AI Pro Support** — in roadmap
- **First payment email** — triggered 30 min after "Pro First Job Completed" event fires; email explains payment timeline. Note: likely going to spam / not primary inbox; candidate for supplemental SMS or link to explainer article
- **Pro app IA for discoverability** — in planning
- **Product and design exploration on Pro problem reporting flows** — in progress
- **Scenario-based video tutorials** — available on Bettermode
- **DLA training conversations** — discussion underway about what's permissible on training content

---

## What Success Looks Like

Metrics to track if this opportunity area is addressed:

- Higher % of Pros claiming at least one job (when offered any)
- Onboarded-to-first-service-complete %
- Lower % of support tickets from new Pros
- Higher average ratings on jobs completed by new Pros
- New Pro NPS — checkpoint-based (pre-first job, first job, second job)
- First Job Claimed / First Job Completed ratio
- Reduction in % of incomplete jobs for new Pros
- Improvement in review quality on both sides of the marketplace
- Training engagement metrics
- Increase in adoption of features surfaced during training
- Higher % of services completed on time with 5-star reviews
- Pros paid sooner or have confirmation payment is coming after first job (reference: DoorDash pays same day)
- Fewer customer disputes after first job completion
- "Best Practices Followed" % — Pros who completed the SOP steps believed critical to success (e.g., correct messaging when claiming, multi-cut protocol for long grass, backup equipment plan). Not currently defined or measured.

## Opportunity 1 — Job Scope & Property Information

Pros cannot accurately evaluate a job before committing to it. Information gaps at claim time cause lost money, disputes, and equipment damage.

| Sub-opportunity | n | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| **1a** | 14 | Pros need terrain, slope, and access information when evaluating a new job so they can avoid losing money on properties that don't match their equipment |
| **1b** | 5 | Pros need customer-supplied photos of the property when claiming so they can calibrate effort against payout before committing |
| **1c** | 7 | Pros need a measurement they trust when sizing up a job so they can stop pricing on unreliable square-footage data |
| **1d** | 7 | Pros need verified information about customer-stated conditions (grass height, debris, dogs, sticks, last cleanup) when claiming so they can plan equipment, time, and price against truth instead of self-reported guesses |
| **1e** | 4 | Pros need property-line and boundary clarity when arriving at a property so they can mow the right yard without getting disputes from neighbors or the customer |
| **1f** | 6 | Pros need a wider claim-evaluation window when a job appears so they can read details before deciding instead of reflex-claiming and discovering scope on arrival |
| **1g** *(p4)* | 3 | Pros need parking guidance for the property when claiming a job so they can plan trailer-and-truck access without damaging their own vehicles or losing time on neighbor disputes |

## Opportunity 2 — Tier & Penalty Fairness

The tier system penalizes Pros for events outside their control. Pros change behavior — including marking jobs falsely complete — to avoid penalties they consider unjust.

| Sub-opportunity | n | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| **2a** | 8 | Pros need customer-initiated cancellations and reschedules to be tracked separately from pro-initiated ones so they can stop being downgraded for events the customer caused |
| **2b** | 7 | Pros need weather and equipment exceptions to be tracked separately from voluntary cancellations so they can request rain reschedules or report a broken mower without losing tier and job flow |
| **2c** | 6 | Pros need to be able to report customer-refused-at-the-door without taking a tier hit so they can stop falsely marking jobs complete to protect themselves |
| **2d** | 6 | Pros need the system's tier/penalty rules to be explicit and discoverable when they are working so they can stop learning the rules through punishment |
| **2e** | 4 | Pros need their disputed first-cut/long-grass payments protected by photo evidence so they can claim the upcharge without fearing customer chargeback |
| **2f** *(p4)* | 1 | Pros need an explicit way to flag pre-existing property damage on arrival so they can stop fearing they will be blamed (and have their pay clawed back) for damage they didn't cause |

## Opportunity 3 — Job Notifications & Account Alerts

Pros miss jobs and lose income because notifications don't fire reliably and account state changes happen silently.

| Sub-opportunity | n | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| **3a** | 9 | Pros need real-time alerts that actually fire when they sign up to receive them so they can claim jobs without compulsive app checking |
| **3b** | 2 | Pros need email and SMS links to deep-link straight into the in-app claim flow when a job hits so they can claim without losing the race to a deep-link bug |
| **3c** | 2 | Pros need the app to recover gracefully when a Pro accidentally STOPs SMS or has a notification toggle bug so they can keep getting jobs without escalating to support |
| **3d** | 4 | Pros need to know the real-time state of their account (paused, suspended, restricted) when it changes so they can fix it before missing a week of jobs |
| **3e** | 2 | Pros need notice when the system makes an automated change to their schedule (auto-rebook, customer reassignment) so they can react instead of finding out at the door |

---

## Opportunity 4 — App Reliability & Flow Completion

Critical in-app flows break at moments that cost Pros money or tier score. Broken flows leave Pros with no recovery path.

| Sub-opportunity | n | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| **4a** | 7 | Pros need customer-initiated cancellations to propagate to their schedule before they leave for the property so they can stop driving 25–40 minutes for nothing |
| **4b** | 5 | Pros need the platform to prevent dispatching the same job to two Pros so they can stop arriving to find someone else mowing the lawn they accepted |
| **4c** | 8 | Pros need flows that fail loudly with a recovery path (reschedule submit, photo upload, complete-job, drop-job) so they can resolve issues without ending up with phantom uncompleted jobs that ding their tier |
| **4d** | 3 | Pros need before-photos to persist across sessions and the in-app camera to actually work so they can capture proof without losing progress |
| **4e** | 1 | Pros need offline support for completing a job so connectivity dead spots at customer properties don't cost them the completion |
| **4f** *(p4)* | 1 | Pros need the in-app reschedule date picker to remain flexible after a previous reschedule so they can move a job earlier when the customer asks, instead of being locked to the new date |

---

## Opportunity 5 — Pay & Pricing Fairness

Pros cannot earn what the actual job is worth. Pricing gaps cause Pros to avoid certain job types, absorb losses, or leave the platform.

| Sub-opportunity | n | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| **5a** | 8 | Pros need pay that reflects the actual work conditions (slope, edging length, debris, terrain difficulty) so they can stop declining jobs that look right by square-footage but are uneconomic in reality |
| **5b** | 7 | Pros need long-grass and overgrown-yard claims to actually compensate them for the time and equipment risk so they can stop avoiding the long-grass feature entirely |
| **5c** | 6 | Pros need distance and route awareness in the job feed when claiming so they can avoid lone, far-out jobs that net negative after gas and time |
| **5d** | 7 | Pros need to know what the customer was charged when they see what they're paid so they can stop feeling exploited and stop quitting the platform |
| **5e** | 6 | Pros need a working in-app re-quote flow so they can capture extra revenue for additional services without resorting to free work or off-platform side deals |
| **5f** *(p4)* | 3 | Pros need a working in-app price-renegotiation path on accepted jobs whose scope exceeds the listed pay so they can keep the job (and the tier credit) without absorbing the loss or calling the customer off-platform |
| **5g** *(p4)* | 1 | Pros on quote-based jobs need a price floor or budget transparency at quote time so they can stop bidding themselves into unprofitable work |
