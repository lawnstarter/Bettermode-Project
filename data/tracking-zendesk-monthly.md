## What Success Looks Like

Per the strategy doc:
- **long_grass_problem:** -15% within 60 days of content going live
- **scheduling_problem + service_help (unresponsive/cancel):** -20% within 60 days
- **account_help (tier confusion):** -20% within 90 days

These are directional targets. A single month of data isn't enough — look for a trend over 2–3 months.

## Notes

- Baseline covers new-tier Pros only (Blue/Intro/Red). Try to maintain the same filter when pulling monthly updates — ask support managers to segment by tier if possible.
- If Zendesk categories shift or new subcategories are added, note it in the monthly row.
- Seasonal effects: ticket volume typically rises in April–June (peak mowing season). A flat number vs. baseline during peak = improvement; a drop = strong improvement.
