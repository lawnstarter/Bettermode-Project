# Off-Platform Pro Success — Community Signals
Source: Bettermode Ask the Community feed
Collected: April 21, 2026
Purpose: Stakeholder brief for Marketing & Growth

---

## The ask

One of the stated goals for the Bettermode community is helping Pros be successful beyond LawnStarter — for their own businesses, not just on the platform. This document captures what Pros are already asking about in that direction so Marketing & Growth has community evidence to act on.

---

## What Pros are asking

### Google Business Profile — 2 direct posts

"Reviews for Google?"
> "Can we send our Google link to customers in messages to have them write reviews for our company? I am trying to be at the top of the Google algorithm for my area."

"Reviews" (Arely Elrod, top 5 all-time community contributor)
> "Can we get a layout of our reviews like Google? How many stars on average does our company get out of how many reviews. I want to be able to share that with possible customers."

HQ responded to the second one: "At the moment, I can't confirm any built-in export feature or a summary view similar to Google's. But your suggestion makes sense, and I'll pass this along to the Product Team as a feature request."

What both Pros want: a way to take their LawnStarter reputation and use it to grow off-platform. One is trying to rank locally. One wants shareable social proof for prospective customers.

---

### Equipment and tools — 1 direct post

"What's Your Go-To Tool Brand (and Which Brand You'll Never Touch Again)?"
A Tips & Tricks thread asking Pros to share equipment recommendations. Milwaukee and Ryobi came up most. Off-topic from software, but shows Pros will actively engage on "what do you use to run your business" questions — the format works.

---

### Off-platform marketing awareness — 1 signal

"Promotion overdrive?"
> "Is there a time when LawnStarter promotes more than other times? Summer more than winter? Can we get a heads up about the trends of promoting. I do see ads on Craigslist, Nextdoor, and other platforms. Just curious to start taking more jobs."

This Pro is watching where LawnStarter advertises and thinking about how to show up in the same spaces for their own business. Not a direct ask, but a clear signal of interest in marketing their own company.

---

### Platform-as-launchpad — organic Pro advice

Scott Culala (top all-time community contributor, 1.35K engagement points) tells this to new Pros repeatedly:
> "I use this app to get into neighborhoods. Then I book the neighbors."
> "Use this tool to help you grow. Or don't."

He frames LawnStarter as a customer acquisition engine for a Pro's personal business, not just a job source. This framing already exists in the community — it just isn't something LawnStarter has formally leaned into.

Hector Alvarez (posted as "Mayan Environmental and Property Services") asked whether LawnStarter is shutting down — not as a complaint, but as genuine business planning:
> "As a small business owner, the responsibility ultimately falls on me to continue providing reliable service."

He's running a real company that depends on the platform and is thinking about diversification. That's a Pro LawnStarter should want to help succeed off-platform.

---

## What this tells marketing and growth

The clearest stated need is Google Business Profile — two Pros asked about it directly, with different angles (local SEO and shareable social proof). That's the easiest entry point.

The broader need is implicit but consistent: Pros want to grow their own business and they see LawnStarter as a tool to do that, not just a job source. There's no existing content on the platform that helps them do this.

No one is asking about CRM software, invoicing tools, or scheduling software in the community — at least not yet. The Google/off-platform reputation angle is where the demand is expressed and where a content play or product conversation would land.

---

## Suggested starting points

1. A community post (or Pro Academy article) on setting up and optimizing a Google Business Profile — written for someone who mows lawns and isn't a marketer
2. A way for Pros to export or share a summary of their LawnStarter ratings (referenced directly in an HQ reply as a feature request already passed to product)
3. A broader "growing your business beyond LawnStarter" content thread — the community will engage; Scott Culala will answer half the questions himself

- The community has 8,320 members and 325 active per week. Small absolute numbers but a concentrated audience of business owners.
