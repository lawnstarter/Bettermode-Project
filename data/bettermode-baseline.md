- **Community activity is trending up sharply:** page views +19.4%, replies +167.57%, new members +14.86% week over week. Something is driving engagement right now (possibly the payout update post, which is the most viewed post this week at 151 views).
- **Metrics content dominates all-time engagement:** Performance metrics, METRICS MAYHEM, and related posts hold 4 of the top 6 spots by replies and 3 of the top 6 by reactions. The First 60 Days and Tier Clarity content series land directly in the highest-demand category.
- **"How Jobs Are Assigned" already has 913 views all-time** — validates that task 7 (getting your first job / queue explainer) will find a ready audience.
- **No tier/rewards explainer in the top 10 all-time** — task 17 fills a gap that clearly exists given the metrics discussion volume.
- **Admin accounts for 2,170 of 6,750 total all-time activities (32%)** — Pros are engaged but the community still leans heavily on admin contribution. Reducing that ratio is one goal of the new content strategy.
