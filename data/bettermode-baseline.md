## How to Pull These Numbers

All three are in **Bettermode Admin → Analytics** (top nav when logged in as admin).

---

### Number 1 — Total active members

**Where:** Admin → Analytics → Members → "Active Members" or "Total Members"

Look for:
- Total registered members (all-time)
- Monthly active members (logged in or engaged in the last 30 days)

Both numbers are useful. Monthly active is the more meaningful baseline for the engagement targets in the strategy.

> **Pull:** Total members + monthly active members. Note the date range.

---

### Number 2 — New Pro members (if filterable)

**Where:** Admin → Analytics → Members → filter by join date (last 90 days) or member tag if Pros are tagged by segment

If Bettermode has member tagging or segments (e.g., "new Pro," "joined < 90 days"), pull that subset. If not, total member count is the denominator.

> **Pull:** New member count (joined in last 90 days) if available. If not, note that the denominator is total members.

---

### Number 3 — Average post views

**Where:** Admin → Analytics → Content → Posts, sorted by views

Pull at two levels:
1. **Overall average** — total views across all posts ÷ total posts (or use whatever average the dashboard shows)
2. **Per-space average** — specifically the "Pro Academy" space and "Ask the Community" space if broken out separately

The strategy doc targets:
- Field Guide posts: 25 unique views per post within 30 days of publishing
- Tier content: 20%+ of community members view at least one tier post/month

To measure those, you need to know the starting point. What are posts getting today with no targeted content strategy?

> **Pull:** Average views per post (overall). If you can pull by space, get Pro Academy and Ask the Community separately.

---

### Bonus — Top 5 posts by views (optional but useful)

If it's easy: pull the top 5 posts by view count. This tells you what's already resonating and gives a ceiling to benchmark against.
