## How to Pull

1. Go to Bettermode Admin → Reports → Posts
2. Set Timeframe: **All time** (so view counts are cumulative, not just last 7 days)
3. Scroll to **Top posts by: View**
4. Record view counts for each tracked post below
5. Note date pulled

Takes ~5 minutes. Do it the same day each week (Monday works well).

## What Success Looks Like

Per the strategy doc:
- **Field Guide posts:** 25+ unique views per post within 30 days of publishing
- **Tier content:** 20%+ of community members view at least one tier post per month
  - Current active members/week: 325 → monthly ~1,300 unique
  - 20% threshold: ~260 members
  - Measure: combined views on tier-related posts (tasks 9, 10, 17) in a given month

---

## Notes

- Bettermode reports **total views**, not unique views. The 25-views target uses total views as a proxy — at this community size they're close enough.
- If a post gets pinned or featured, note it — that artificially boosts views and shouldn't be compared directly to unpinned posts.
- The payout update post ("💰 Payout Update: Mowing Prices Are Increasing") hit 151 views in 7 days — it was an announcement, not evergreen content. Don't use announcement posts as benchmarks for content posts.
- For the monthly tier engagement calc: pull the combined view total for tasks 9, 10, 17 posts each month and divide by that month's active member count.
